About RoomieMatch

RoomieMatch is an hostel roommate finder specially designed for Chitkara University students.

This platform helps students find compatible roommates using hobbies, study patterns, gaming habits, sports interests, music taste and lifestyle compatibility.

Our goal is to reduce roommate conflicts and improve hostel life.
